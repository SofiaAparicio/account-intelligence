How accounts are ranked

Every account gets two scores from 0 to 100. The risk score is how much evidence there is that it is in trouble; the opportunity score is how much evidence there is that it wants to buy more. Each score is built from five checks: seat usage, days since the last sales contact, open support tickets, AI feature adoption, and what was said on the last call. Lists are ordered by urgency — the score, how soon the account renews, and how much revenue is at stake — so a large account renewing next month can rank above a smaller one with a higher score. Open any account to see every check behind its score.
